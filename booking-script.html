Operating rules

Three things to remember while booking. They will save your day if anything goes sideways.

01
Speed beats optimization
Don't agonize over a 1:30 PM vs 2:00 PM return time. Tap whatever's offered first — you can modify it later. Every second you spend choosing is a second Rise of the Resistance Single Pass is closer to selling out.
02
Modify, never cancel
If you want a different return time, tap "Modify" — your spot stays in queue. Tapping "Cancel" releases it to the public pool and you lose priority. Same end result, very different odds of getting what you want.
03
After the initial drop, refresh-stalk
Once your 4 days are booked, spend 5–10 minutes hitting Modify on each booking to see if earlier times have opened up. People cancel within minutes of booking. This is free time-travel for your day.
