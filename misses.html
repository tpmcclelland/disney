⚠ Dining reservations · Act this week

The 60-day window closed April 9

Disney's advance dining reservation window opens 60 days before your trip start. For June 8, that meant April 9 — months ago. You missed the prime booking window for any table-service restaurant.

The good news: cancellations roll in constantly, especially within the 7-day window before each reservation date. Refresh-stalking the My Disney Experience app daily can land you reservations even this late. Walk-up availability also opens throughout each park day.

01
Open the My Disney Experience app daily this week. Dining → search your dates → check top picks. Cancellations drop most often 24–72 hours before the reservation.
02
Set up MouseDining (mousedining.com) or DVC Reservation Finder. They text/email you when slots open for restaurants you flagged. Most are free for limited use.
03
During each park day, check Walk-Up Available in MDX. It refreshes throughout the day and shows immediate-availability tables — sometimes for spots you can't book in advance.
04
Lunch is easier than dinner. Character meals (Tusker House, Crystal Palace) often have walk-ups available. Signature spots (Sci-Fi Dine-In, Space 220, Be Our Guest) are the hardest.

Tuesday · June 9 · Animal Kingdom

Animal Kingdom

Two whole areas being skipped: DinoLand (in transition to Tropical Americas) and Rafiki's Planet Watch. Several quality shows and the iconic Pandora at dusk experience also miss the schedule.

Areas being skipped entirely

DinoLand U.S.A. — currently being transformed into a new "Tropical Americas" land. DINOSAUR thrill ride and most attractions may be closed during construction. Check status day-of, but it's not a big loss in current state.

Rafiki's Planet Watch — Wildlife Express train, Conservation Station, animal viewing, petting area. Quiet corner of the park; 45-90 min commitment if you go.

Attractions not on the plan

5 attractions

Pandora lights up after sunset — bioluminescent paint, glowing plants, magical sky effects. Truly stunning photo opportunity. Park closes early Tuesday (7-8 PM), but if you stayed until just before close, you'd see it. Pushes the day later.
After sunset

Thursday · June 11 · Magic Kingdom

Magic Kingdom

Your biggest-skip park — MK has ~25 attractions, you're hitting maybe 8-10. The pattern is Fantasyland-heavy, Adventureland-light. Two whole lands barely get touched. The dining scene here is iconic.

Lands you'll barely touch

Adventureland — Pirates of the Caribbean, Jungle Cruise, Tiki Room, Magic Carpets of Aladdin, Treehouse. Pirates is the biggest single miss in this section. Liberty Square beyond Haunted Mansion — Liberty Square Riverboat, Hall of Presidents (50-min animatronic show). Most of Frontierland — Big Thunder Mountain (kids' sub, on rebook list), Tiana's Bayou Adventure (kids' pick, on rebook list), Tom Sawyer Island, Country Bear Jamboree.

Friday · June 12 · EPCOT

EPCOT

Skipping one whole pavilion (The Seas) plus the Imagination pavilion. Most missing rides are second-tier, but the dining at EPCOT is the deepest at WDW — 11 country pavilions means 25+ restaurants you won't visit.

Pavilions being skipped

The Seas with Nemo & Friends pavilion — entire pavilion with the ride, Turtle Talk with Crush, manatees, sharks, real aquarium. Under-rated for a family day. ~45-60 min for everything inside. The Imagination pavilion — Figment ride, Pixar Short Film Festival, Awesome Planet film. Quirky but low-priority.

Attractions not on the plan

8+ attractions
★Turtle Talk with Crush
Crush from Finding Nemo talks live with kids in the audience. Actual real-time conversations. AC, ~15 min, walk-in usually. The Seas pavilion. Kid favorite. Worth a detour during the World Nature stop.
Walk-in · AC
★Mission: SPACE · Green version
Two versions: Green (mild, family-friendly) vs. Orange (intense G-forces, can cause nausea). Green is fine for the 7-year-old. 40″ min. ~30 min standby with Multi Pass rebook available.
40″ · Green
★The American Adventure
35-min animatronic show about American history. AC, sit-down, surprisingly moving. America pavilion. Adult-leaning but kids handle it. Walk-in.
35 min · Walk-in

6-min ride with the iconic purple dragon. Low wait. AC. Adults find it nostalgic; kids find it weird in a fun way.
