Tuesday · June 9 Hours · 8 AM – 7 PM Park day · 02 of 04

Animal Kingdom

Shortest park day with the best timing. Morning LL block (9:35–11:35), Pandora afternoon, walking trails to close. Bonus: you actually got Multi Pass here — gives the day way more structure.

★ Today's mission

Kilimanjaro Safaris at rope drop (only headliner without LL coverage, plus animals are most active in morning heat). Then ride your booked Lightning Lanes back-to-back.

Thursday · June 11 Hours · 8:30 AM – 11 PM Park day · 03 of 04

Magic Kingdom

The big one. Five Lightning Lane bookings spread across the day, fireworks finale. Pacing reset on Wednesday matters here — this is a 14-hour push and worth doing right.

★ Today's mission

Peter Pan's Flight at rope drop (only Tier 1 ride not on LL, and the worst standby at MK by far). Then follow the LL ladder all the way to Seven Dwarfs Mine Train at 7:50 — set up perfectly for Happily Ever After fireworks at 9.

6:45 AM
Wake
Wake, breakfast, sunscreen
Earliest wake of the trip. MK requires transport (ferry, monorail, or bus) — extra time needed.

8:00 AM
Travel

8:20 AM

8:30 AM
Rope drop

No running. Through Main Street, around castle, to Fantasyland. Peter Pan is the most-skipped rope drop and the highest standby ride at MK.
8:45 AM

9:15 AM

Friday · June 12 Hours · 9 AM – 9 PM Park day · 04 of 04 · Finale

EPCOT

Trip-closing day. Front-half rides until 11 (World Showcase opens then), festival booth grazing in the afternoon, Test Track at 5, Luminous fireworks at 9. The most relaxed pacing of any park day.

★ Today's mission

Soarin' Around the World at rope drop (the classic — your booked LL is for Soarin' Across America, the NEW film). Then ride your LLs back-to-back through the afternoon. Rebook Remy's after using Spaceship Earth.

Morning · front half rides
7:30 AM
Wake
Wake, breakfast, sunscreen
Park opens at 9. You can sleep slightly later than other days.
8:15 AM
Travel
Leave hotel for EPCOT

8:30 AM
Travel
Arrive EPCOT, security

8:55 AM
Rope drop
Walk to Soarin' (World Nature)
Past Spaceship Earth, then left into World Nature. ~8 min walk. Soarin' is in The Land pavilion.
9:10 AM
Standby
Soarin' Around the World (original)

Position

9:00 PM
Fireworks

17-minute fireworks + fountain + projection show across the lagoon. The trip-closing moment.

Exit

Sleep
Back at hotel — last bedtime of the trip
