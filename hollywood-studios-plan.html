Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets

The Aerosmith coaster reimagined with The Electric Mayhem. Same track and launch (0–57 mph in 2.8 seconds, three inversions), totally new story and music. Expect peak waits in the first two months of opening — your June trip is right in that window. Plan accordingly.

Lightning Lane Strategy

Multi Pass + Rise Single Pass. Sequence the two big coasters carefully.

Hollywood Studios is where Multi Pass earns its cost the hardest. Five rides routinely run 60+ min standby. Without Multi Pass, you'll do two of them and call it a day. Rise of the Resistance gets the Single Pass treatment — there's no rope drop discount on it the way there is at other parks.

Your first Multi Pass advance pick is the key decision: Slinky Dog Dash or the new Muppets coaster. Pick whichever you're least likely to rope drop. Rope drop the other one if you can; if not, accept the standby for one of them.

Multi Pass$35 / PERSON Rise Single Pass$22 / PERSON Family of 5 total$285

The plan

Right-side sweep starting at Sunset Boulevard, working counter-clockwise through Toy Story Land, Galaxy's Edge, and back toward Echo Lake. Plan assumes Multi Pass advance pick = Slinky Dog Dash and you rope drop the Muppets coaster. Reverse if you'd rather sleep in.

HS-specific notes

Things that matter at this park specifically.

Muppets coaster is brand new

Opened May 26, 2026. First two months mean novelty crowds + new-ride bugs. Expect 100+ min standby and occasional unscheduled closures. Multi Pass priority OR rope drop are the only sane plays.

Rope drop doesn't help Rise

Unlike other Disney headliners, Rise of the Resistance waits don't drop dramatically at park open — 100 min at rope drop, still 60+ at close. Single Pass is essentially required. Save your rope drop energy for Slinky Dog or Muppets coaster.

Limited shade, more concrete

HS has less shade than AK or EPCOT. Toy Story Land and Galaxy's Edge are both heavily paved/open. Bring hats and use any indoor air-conditioned space (Star Tours queue, Tower lobby, Indy theater) as breaks.

Fantasmic dining package

Book a Fantasmic Dining Package at Hollywood & Vine, Mama Melrose, or The Hollywood Brown Derby — includes meal + guaranteed Fantasmic seating in a reserved section. Saves 60 min of seat-holding.

Construction zones

The former Muppet*Vision area (closed June 2025) is walled off for Monsters Inc. land construction. Animation Courtyard partially repurposed for The Walt Disney Studios expansion. Affects navigation in the park's center.

Skip Park Hopping in

Hollywood Studios fills earliest in the day and has the longest standby times on property. Park Hopping out of HS to EPCOT in the afternoon makes sense (catch Luminous fireworks). Park Hopping into HS afternoon means you missed every short wait window.
