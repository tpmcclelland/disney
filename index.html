The high-leverage moments

Five things you cannot forget

01
7:00 AM, every park day
Wake up. Open My Disney Experience. At 7:00:00 sharp, tap into your Multi Pass booking and Single Pass purchase for that day's headliner. On EPCOT day (Friday), have multiple phones ready on the Cosmic Rewind Virtual Queue page — it sells out in seconds.
02
Sunday night prep ritual
Verify all 5 family tickets are linked in MDX. Payment method saved. Phone unlocked, app open, biometric login confirmed. Set 6:55 AM alarm for Monday's 7 AM booking.
03
Refresh-stalk dining all week
Open MDX → Dining → search your dates each morning. Cancellations open up slots daily. Sci-Fi Dine-In, 50's Prime Time, Be Our Guest, Le Cellier are the targets.
04
Mobile order every quick service
Pecos Bill, Satu'li Canteen, Docking Bay 7, Regal Eagle — all in the MDX app. Pick a pickup window 30 min ahead. Saves 15–30 min per meal for a family of 5.
05
Single Rider lines are free time
Test Track + Remy's at EPCOT, Smugglers Run at HS. At Epic Universe Saturday, Mario Kart + Stardust Racers + Hiccup's all have Single Rider. Cuts 60–100 min waits down to 15–25.
